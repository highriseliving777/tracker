How the 90/180 rule works (same for Schengen & Turkey, US citizens): On any given day, look back exactly 180 days. Count every day you were physically inside the zone during that window — both your entry day and exit day count as full days, and even one minute past midnight counts the whole day. You may stay max 90 days in any rolling 180-day window. This tool uses the official EU "look-back" method (same logic as the European Commission's short-stay calculator and helloschengen.com).

⚠️ This is a personal aid, not legal advice. Always verify critical dates with the official EU Short-Stay Calculator before travel.
